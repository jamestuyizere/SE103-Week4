Problem:
I want to prepare my laptop and study materials before starting a study session. I need to make sure that everything I need is ready so I can study without unnecessary interruptions.

Input:
A laptop, a working internet connection, a study topic, and the required learning materials.

Output:
A ready study setup where the laptop is working, the required materials are available, and I can begin studying.

Edge Case Handled:
If the internet connection is not working, I will use downloaded materials or reconnect to the network before starting the study session.

Pseudocode:
START
1. Turn on the laptop.
2. Check that the laptop has enough battery power.
3. Open the required study materials.
4. Check the internet connection.
5. IF the internet connection is working THEN
       Open the online learning resources.
   ELSE
       Use downloaded study materials or reconnect to the network.
6. Choose the topic I will study.
7. Arrange the required materials so they are easy to access.
8. Start the study session.
END

Why this is clear enough to follow:
The steps are written in a specific order so that I know what to do first and what to do next. The input explains what must be ready before the process begins. The IF/ELSE decision explains what to do when the internet is unavailable instead of leaving the situation unclear. The output also explains what successful completion of the process looks like.
